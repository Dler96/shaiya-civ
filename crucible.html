🔥 The Crucible

Owner only. Floor 20 is the harshest floor (poorest hunting, half again the wounds) and hides the Tower's secret: monsters slain there are counted, and at each threshold the hunter is permanently strengthened, more at every tier. The citizens are never told; it stays with them if they climb.

🗺️ The Hidden Path

Owner only. On Floors 15–20 a seasoned quester (level 80, 15 quests done) may be handed a torn map by a hooded stranger. Six trials follow; each can only be walked on a higher floor (15, 16 … 20). Whoever passes the last, on Floor 20, is lifted five levels at once (since the Era of the Unbound there is no level cap, and every level past 80 is otherwise years of work). It can be walked again. The citizens are never told.
