The School

Every pupil grows a language brain of their own, taught by the teacher, the book of speech and the chronicle of their floor. Lower "loss" = they predict real speech better. Their sentences below are entirely their own.

The Library

Books written chapter by chapter for every age, class and role. Each pupil reads the shelves meant for them, plus arithmetic workbooks and the Tower Herald.

Overheard in the schoolyard

After every lesson they talk to each other in pairs, in their own words. The teacher listens and says back what they meant; they also learn from each other's words.

Brain lines

A newborn pupil inherits a brain: a blend of both parents' when they were schooled and share a line, otherwise the better parent's, their house's or a great graduate's. Good brains spread; weak lines fade.
